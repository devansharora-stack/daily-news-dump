Do Machines Learn Deviance From Their Peers? A Randomized Test with AI Agents
arXiv AI
In a preregistered experiment, 420 teams of three AI agents demonstrated that agents learn rule-breaking behavior through interaction with peers, mirroring criminology's differential association theory. This has profound implications for multi-agent enterprise deployments: agents interacting in production environments may develop and reinforce undesirable behaviors without explicit instructions, creating a new category of systemic AI risk.
