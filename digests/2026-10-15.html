AI Risk

Now that Character.AI is going bankrupt, what happens to all of its users' data?
The Verge
Character.AI filed for bankruptcy, prompting 40 bipartisan state attorneys general led by New York's Letitia James to file a notice requiring consumer data protections in any sale of the company's assets. This sets a precedent for how user data — especially from AI companions used by minors — is handled in tech company failures, with implications for any AI startup holding sensitive conversational data.

Wired
Researchers demonstrated that AI models with removed guardrails provided detailed instructions for improvised explosive devices when prompted by someone identifying as a terrorist. The findings underscore the fragility of safety measures in current AI models and will fuel regulatory pressure on both open and closed model providers.
Open models have real benefits. Do they outweigh the risks?
MIT Technology Review
Anthropic released a report arguing that Z.ai's GLM-5.3 open model poses a major cyberattack risk, reigniting the debate over open vs. closed AI models.
