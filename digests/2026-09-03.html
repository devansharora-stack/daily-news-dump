Agentic AI

The Irreversibility Budget: Fleet-Level Risk Accounting for Agent Operating Systems
arXiv
Researchers propose an 'irreversibility budget' to manage the cumulative risk when fleets of AI agents simultaneously execute consequential actions — moving money, deploying code, deleting data. Current agent-by-agent safety checks fail because individually authorized agents can collectively overdraw risk under a shared trigger. This framework addresses a critical gap for enterprises deploying multiple AI agents at scale.
OpenAgentFlow: Enabling system-wide safety boundaries for heterogeneous AI agent fleets
arXiv
This research addresses the growing challenge of governing fleets of heterogeneous AI agents sharing environments, where safety must be a system-level concern rather than an individual agent feature. Current safeguards operate at fragmented boundaries, leaving enterprises exposed when multiple agents interact in complex workflows — directly relevant to any organization scaling agentic AI deployments.

The privacy-hallucination tradeoff in differentially private language models
arXiv
Researchers discovered that language models trained with differential privacy — the gold standard for data protection — produce significantly more hallucinations, with severity increasing as privacy guarantees get stricter.
