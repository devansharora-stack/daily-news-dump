Research reveals LLM-as-a-Judge is unreliable — self-improving AI agents need deterministic guardrails
arXiv AI
A new paper argues that using LLMs to judge other LLMs in self-improving agent pipelines is fundamentally flawed, as the judge 'has not earned' its oracle status. The authors propose demoting LLM judges to advisors gated by deterministic verification layers. For enterprises deploying self-optimizing AI systems, this is a critical warning about compounding errors in autonomous workflows.
