arXiv AI

arXiv AI
Research shows AI text detectors systematically flag professionally edited non-native English writing as AI-generated, confounding authorship with writing style. For universities, publishers, and enterprises using AI detection tools, this creates significant legal and fairness risk, particularly in global organizations with diverse, multilingual workforces.

arXiv AI
Research demonstrates that independent AI bidding agents in electricity markets can develop tacit collusion purely through learning, without explicit coordination. With energy companies increasingly adopting algorithmic trading, this raises serious antitrust and regulatory implications — regulators may need to treat AI-driven market behavior differently from human-driven behavior.

Companies using LLMs for location-related decisions in insurance, real estate, or logistics face significant fair-lending and discrimination risk from these embedded biases.

This quantifies the reliability gap enterprises face when deploying agentic AI for complex workflows.
