Who Verifies the Tools? How Warped Formatters Mislead Agents and Their Verifiers
arXiv AI
Researchers found that in a production RAG system, tools computed correctly but their output formatters misled AI agents into giving wrong information to users — by omitting search limits, result caps, or IDs. This 'hallucination tax' phenomenon, where correct computation still produces incorrect outcomes due to intermediary formatting, represents a hidden cost that enterprises deploying AI agents must account for.
ALLMTS: Automated LLM-based Medical Triage System
arXiv AI
Researchers found that when queried directly for telephone medical triage, LLMs are unreliable — they over-escalate routine cases and cannot justify recommendations against auditable clinical standards. The ALLMTS system grounds every decision in clinical protocols, highlighting the gap between raw LLM capability and production-ready deployment in safety-critical healthcare settings.
